## Do not reopen

Three ClearSign directions were tried in two days: the device decoder (D-008),
per-transaction descriptions signed online (D-009), then back to the device
decoder with no live signing (**D-018, active**). A session at 19:05 on
2026-10-04 proposed per-transaction v1 again and a new "generic decoder",
because it had not seen D-018 and could not find the 7.16 source. Both are
settled:

- **No online key decides what the device shows** (D-018). Per-transaction v1
  stays out of the certified tier; firmware refuses it inside the 0x03
  envelope (the `e44e4999d` gate, restored by `e2c0914d3`).
- **The generic engine already exists.** 7.16 carries an on-device ERC-7730
  interpreter (`lib/firmware/erc7730_program.c`, `erc7730_abi_stream.c`,
  `erc7730_field.c`, `erc7730_format.c`, `erc7730_workflow.c`,
  `erc7730_catalog.c`) and a compiler
  (`deps/python-keepkey/keepkeylib/erc7730_compiler.py`). Extend it; do not
  design a new one.

## Process rules from the retros

- Scope a journey from the dapp's real request (owner's attempt in the
  Desktop log, or live chain traffic), not from deploy files.
- A journey is "wired" only after a run through Desktop's REST path against
  the emulator.
- A known gap on the app's default path is a blocker; never route the owner
  around it, never suggest AdvancedMode as a workaround.
- Report flash and RAM on every firmware change (bootloader limit 655,321 B).
- Before any design: read DECISIONS.md, quote the active decision, say why the
  proposal does not reopen it.
